About

I work on medical imaging AI for mammography in Lunit's Cancer Screening Group. My background is computer vision, especially label-efficient segmentation and detection. Over the past two years my work has moved into multimodal models: pre-training vision-language models on mammogram and report pairs, and fine-tuning multimodal LLMs with LoRA and GRPO. I also build LLM agent systems, including a multi-agent assistant that answers internal questions about our models and datasets.

The problems I enjoy most are the ones where a model meets messy real data and has to be measured honestly: learning from cheap labels, finding where foundation models fail, and making agent failures visible instead of silent. I'm currently most interested in multimodal learning, reinforcement fine-tuning of MLLMs, agentic systems and physical AI.

Before Lunit, I did my M.S. at POSTECH in the Computer Vision Lab with Prof. Suha Kwak.

Experience

  1. Feb 2024 – now

    Applied Research Scientist

    Cancer Screening Group, Lunit, Seoul

    • Building a production mammography positioning quality assessment model (PGMI) based on anatomical landmark detection, from model development through deployment.
    • Benchmarked foundation MLLMs (GPT-5.2, Qwen3-VL, MedGemma and others) zero-shot on image quality assessment; all stayed below Cohen's κ 0.2. Fine-tuning with LoRA SFT and GRPO raised κ by 0.14 over the zero-shot baseline.
    • Co-developed CLIP-style vision-language models on 500K mammogram and report pairs, improving cancer classification AUC from 0.73 to 0.78 and 5-year risk prediction AUC from 0.65 to 0.69 over supervised baselines.
    • Building an LLM multi-agent system that answers questions from QA, regulatory and medical affairs teams about our models and datasets, routing to sub-agents for tool-based database retrieval and Confluence search.
    • Designed a dual mixture-of-experts survival model, improving C-index by up to 0.04 over strong baselines.
